Prepositions in Pictures

Picture flashcards for business English

Get in, on, at and for right at work

180 business preposition patterns such as responsible for, on time and in charge of, one clear picture each, and three example sentences from easy to hard. Nine themes: meetings and presentations, communication, projects and deadlines, finance and money, sales and customers, HR and careers, workplace life, decisions and problem solving, strategy and growth.

  • Flip through cards with the preposition highlighted, and switch between easy, medium and hard sentences.
  • Fill the gap: pick the right preposition from four options.
  • Type each sentence from memory with word-by-word feedback. A phrase counts as learned once all three sentences are typed, and your review pile is one tap away.
  • Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.
